How reminders work

When Medico books a discovery Zoom on Monday (date and time, Zoom link, status Booked), the client gets a confirmation, a reminder 24 hours before and one 1 hour before. When a call is marked No-show, the client gets a "sorry we missed you" email that day and one follow-up a day later. Emails come from Medico's own address. Setting Reminder to Off stops them for that client.
